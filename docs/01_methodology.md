# 01 · Methodology

## Research Design

The study uses a **mixed-methods** approach. Desk research sets the market context. Primary research tests the assumptions that matter most. A financial model turns the findings into economics that can be stress-tested.

```
Hypotheses ──► Evidence (desk + primary) ──► Model ──► Stress test ──► Score ──► Recommendation
     ▲                                                                              │
     └──────────────────────── revise assumptions ◄─────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Data Sources

| Type | Examples | Use |
|------|----------|-----|
| Secondary: industry | Trade association reports, market research summaries | Market size and growth |
| Secondary: government | U.S. Census trade data (HS codes), USDA, FDA guidance | Import volumes, compliance requirements |
| Secondary: public companies | 10-K filings of listed food / CPG companies | Margin and cost benchmarks |
| Secondary: digital | Marketplace listings, search-trend data | Price points, demand signals |
| Primary: customers | 15–20 semi-structured interviews, 100+ response survey | Willingness to pay, purchase behavior |
| Primary: supply side | Supplier, co-packer and freight-forwarder quotes | Landed cost, lead times, MOQs |

All primary data is stored in `confidential/` and reported here only in aggregated, anonymized form.

## Feasibility Dimensions and Weights

| Dimension | Weight | Key question |
|-----------|-------:|--------------|
| Market | 25% | Is demand large, growing and reachable? |
| Financial | 30% | Are the returns attractive relative to the capital and risk? |
| Operational | 20% | Can we source, comply with regulation and deliver reliably? |
| Risk | 15% | Are the downside risks manageable? |
| Organizational | 10% | Can the team execute? |

Each dimension is scored **1–5** against a written rubric (below). The weighted total gives the recommendation:

| Weighted score | Recommendation |
|---------------:|----------------|
| ≥ 3.75 | **Go** |
| 3.00 – 3.74 | **Go with conditions** (specific milestones must be met first) |
| < 3.00 | **No-go**, or re-scope |

**Knock-out rule:** a score of 1 in any single dimension means no-go, whatever the weighted total.

## Scoring Rubric (summary)

| Score | Market | Financial | Operational |
|------:|--------|-----------|-------------|
| 5 | Segment growing faster than 8% a year, strong willingness-to-pay (WTP) evidence | NPV well above 0 in the downside case, break-even within 18 months | Multiple qualified suppliers, clear compliance path |
| 3 | Moderate growth, mixed WTP evidence | NPV above 0 in the base case only | One qualified supplier, manageable compliance gaps |
| 1 | Flat or declining, weak WTP | NPV below 0 in the base case | No reliable supply, or compliance blockers |

## Financial Analysis Standards

- **Horizon:** 5 years, annual periods
- **Discount rate:** 15% to 20% (early-stage venture hurdle rate); 18% in the base case
- **Scenarios:** Downside / Base / Upside, each with an explicit set of assumptions
- **Sensitivity:** one-at-a-time ±20% on price, unit cost, volume and CAC
- **Metrics:** contribution margin, break-even units, NPV, IRR, payback period, LTV:CAC

## Limitations

- Survey sample sizes are small and drawn from a convenience sample. Results are directional, not statistically representative.
- Supplier quotes are point-in-time and exposed to FX and freight volatility.
- Published market estimates differ in how they define the category, so this study uses ranges rather than single figures.
