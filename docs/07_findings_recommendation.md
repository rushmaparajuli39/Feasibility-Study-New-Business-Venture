## Preliminary Recommendation: **Go with conditions**

The total score of 3.10 falls in the 3.00–3.74 band. Proceed to a **limited, capped pilot**, and move to full launch only if all of the following are met:

| # | Condition | Target | Evidence source |
|---|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| C1 | Validated price point | Survey's acceptable price range includes the modeled price | Survey (Van Westendorp) |
| C2 | Acquisition efficiency | Pilot CAC at or below the modeled value | Paid pilot results |
| C3 | Supply redundancy | 2 suppliers scoring 3.5 or higher | Supplier scorecards |
| C4 | Clean first import | First shipment clears entry with no detention | Customs / broker records |

## What Would Change the Recommendation

- **Toward Go:** WTP supports a 10% higher price, or repeat-order share reaches 45% or more. Either one moves the base case clearly above the hurdle rate.
- **Toward No-go:** pilot CAC comes in more than 25% above the model, or no second supplier can be qualified.
