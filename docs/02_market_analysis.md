# 02 · Market Analysis

> Figures are **illustrative ranges** used to show the method. Validated numbers and their sources are held privately.

## Category Definition

The relevant category is **premium specialty food ingredients**: differentiated, often single-origin products sold at a price premium over mass-market equivalents. They are bought by:

- Home cooks who are interested in food and willing to pay for quality and provenance
- Independent restaurants and specialty food-service operators
- Specialty retailers and gift buyers

## Demand Drivers

| Driver | Direction | Evidence to gather |
|--------|:---------:|--------------------|
| Growing interest in global cuisines | ↑ | Search trends, cookbook / media coverage, menu-incidence data |
| Premiumization of pantry staples | ↑ | Price-tier mix on marketplaces, retailer shelf audits |
| Interest in provenance and ethical sourcing | ↑ | Survey WTP questions, competitor messaging |
| Health and wellness positioning | ↑ | Category growth in "functional" ingredients |
| Household budget pressure | ↓ | Consumer-sentiment data, trade-down behavior |

## Market Sizing (top-down and bottom-up)

### Top-down

| Layer | Definition | Illustrative estimate |
|-------|------------|----------------------:|
| **TAM** | U.S. retail and food-service spend in the broader category | $X.X B |
| **SAM** | Premium / specialty tier reachable through online and specialty channels | ~10–15% of TAM |
| **SOM** | Realistic share by year 5, given competition and budget | ~0.5–1.0% of SAM |

### Bottom-up (cross-check)

```
Target households (online specialty buyers)       N
× Annual purchase incidence in category           p
× Orders per buyer per year                       f
× Average order value                             AOV
= Serviceable revenue pool
```

The bottom-up figure is the one used in the financial model. Top-down serves only as a sense check. If the two differ by more than 2×, the assumptions are revisited.

## Customer Segments

| Segment | Needs | Channel | Priority |
|---------|-------|---------|:--------:|
| S1 · Enthusiastic home cooks | Quality, authenticity, recipes / education | DTC website, marketplaces | High |
| S2 · Gift buyers | Presentation, story, convenience | DTC, seasonal retail | Medium |
| S3 · Independent restaurants | Consistency, bulk pricing, reliability | Direct B2B | Medium |
| S4 · Specialty retailers | Margin, sell-through, merchandising support | Wholesale / distributor | Low (year 2+) |

## Primary Research Plan

- **Interviews:** 15–20, split across S1 and S3. See [`templates/customer_interview_guide.md`](../templates/customer_interview_guide.md).
- **Survey:** 100+ responses. Measures willingness to pay with a Van Westendorp price-sensitivity question set. See [`templates/survey_questionnaire.md`](../templates/survey_questionnaire.md).
- **Success thresholds:**
  - ≥ 40% of S1 respondents rate the value proposition 4+ out of 5
  - The acceptable price range overlaps the price assumed in the model
