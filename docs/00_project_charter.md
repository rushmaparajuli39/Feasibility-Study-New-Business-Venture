# 00 · Project Charter

## Background

Project HSV is a proposed venture to bring a curated line of premium specialty ingredients to U.S. consumers and food-service buyers. Before any capital is committed, this study tests whether the opportunity is viable and on what terms.

## Objective

Produce an evidence-based **go / no-go / go-with-conditions** recommendation, supported by market, operational, financial and risk analysis.

## Scope

**In scope**
- U.S. market entry, with direct-to-consumer (DTC) e-commerce and selected B2B channels
- Import, packaging, labeling and fulfillment requirements
- A 5-year financial outlook under three scenarios
- Risk identification and mitigation planning

**Out of scope**
- Detailed brand identity and creative work
- Securing financing (the study informs it but does not include it)
- International markets outside the U.S.

## Key Assumptions to Validate

1. Target customers will pay a premium for provenance, freshness and quality.
2. Landed cost per unit allows a gross margin of 55% or more on DTC.
3. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) can be recovered within 12 months.
4. The supply base can scale to 3× year-1 volume without loss of quality.
