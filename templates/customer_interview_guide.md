# Customer Interview Guide

**Format:** 30 minutes, semi-structured · **Target:** 15–20 interviews (segments S1 and S3)
**Rule:** ask about past behavior, not hypothetical intentions. Don't pitch.

Notes are stored in `confidential/interviews/` as `INT-###.md`. Only coded, anonymized themes go into the public docs.

## 1. Warm-up (3 min)
- Tell me about how you cook / source ingredients for your kitchen.
- How often do you try new ingredients or cuisines?

## 2. Current behavior (10 min)
- Think about the last time you bought a specialty ingredient. What was it, where did you buy it, and why there?
- How did you decide it was good quality?
- What did you pay? Did the price feel fair?
- What frustrates you about buying these products today?

## 3. Value drivers (8 min)
- Rank these by importance: freshness, origin / provenance, price, packaging, convenience, ethical sourcing.
- Have you ever paid more for a product because of where it came from? Tell me about it.

## 4. Concept reaction (6 min)
_Show the neutral concept card (no brand)._
- What stands out? What's unclear?
- What would you expect to pay for this?
- What would stop you from buying it?

## 5. Wrap-up (3 min)
- Anything else I should have asked?
- May I follow up when we run a pilot?
